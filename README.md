# Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

A business analytics project completed as part of the **BCG X Forage virtual experience**, analysing a multi-channel marketing campaign for **NewCo**, a fictional big-box retailer.

The project focused on identifying which campaign and channel combination was most effective for **growing new customer sales**.

## Business Context

NewCo tested two campaign messages across three channels:

* **Campaign A** — informal, conversational tone
* **Campaign B** — sales-focused, promotional tone
* **Channels** — Email, Instagram and Website Banner

The key questions were:

* Which campaign and channel combination performed best?
* What drove new customer engagement?
* Where should marketing investment be focused?

## Analysis & Findings

I analysed campaign data and pre-built Excel pivot tables to compare performance across **channels, campaign messages and customer groups**.

Key findings included:

* **Email** generated the highest sales compared with the other channels.
* **Campaign A** performed strongly when analysing new customer sales.
* Existing customers generated more sales overall than new customers.
* Website Banner produced comparatively lower sales.

Based on the analysis, I identified **Campaign A through Email** as the combination to prioritise for future new customer acquisition within the campaign data.
