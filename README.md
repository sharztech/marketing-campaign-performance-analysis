# Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

A business analytics project completed as part of the **BCG X Forage virtual experience**, analysing the performance of a multi-channel marketing campaign for a fictional big-box retailer, **NewCo**.

The analysis focused on understanding how different campaign messages and marketing channels performed across **new and existing customers**, with a particular focus on identifying opportunities to grow new customer sales.

## Business Context

NewCo had completed a multi-channel marketing campaign testing two different messaging approaches across three marketing channels.

**Campaign A** used an informal, conversational tone, while **Campaign B** used a more sales-focused and promotional tone.

The campaign was delivered through:

* Email
* Instagram
* Website Banner

The key business questions were:

* Which campaign and channel combination delivered the most value?
* What drove engagement from new customers?
* Where should NewCo focus its marketing investment next quarter?

## Analysis

I reviewed campaign performance data and pre-built pivot tables to compare:

* Campaign performance across different channels.
* Sales generated by Campaign A and Campaign B.
* Performance among new and existing customers.
* Differences between total sales and new customer sales.
* Patterns that could inform future marketing investment.

A key finding was that **Email performed strongly compared with the other channels**, while **Website Banner generated comparatively lower sales**.

When focusing specifically on **new customers**, Campaign A's informal messaging showed strong performance across the channels analysed. This indicated that message style could influence how effectively campaigns engage potential new customers.

## Key Insight

The analysis indicated that **Campaign A through Email** was the strongest combination for generating new customer sales within the campaign data.

The findings suggested that:

* Email generated substantially higher sales than the other channels.
* Campaign A performed strongly when analysing new customer activity.
* Existing customers generated more sales overall than new customers.
* Website Banner produced comparatively lower sales than Email and Instagram.

These findings helped shift the focus from overall campaign performance towards the client's specific objective of **growing new customer sales**.

## Recommendation

Based on the analysis, I recommended that NewCo prioritise **Campaign A through Email** when considering future marketing investment aimed at acquiring new customers.

The recommendation was based on the combination's performance in the available campaign data, alongside the stronger results observed for Campaign A among new customers.

## Stakeholder Considerations

The analysis considered the different priorities of stakeholders:

* **Marketing:** Identify which messaging approach could be scaled.
* **Customer Growth:** Understand differences between new and existing customer performance.
* **Business Leadership:** Use campaign results to inform future marketing investment.
